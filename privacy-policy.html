Privacy Policy

Language: English · 简体中文

Effective October 21, 2026 · Applies to Pettrove version 1.1 and later

Pettrove (宠记) is a local-first pet health and care journal. It runs on your iPhone and keeps your records on your device. This policy explains how we handle (or, more accurately, how we do not handle) your data.

1. Data we collect

Pettrove does not collect, upload, or sell any of your personal data. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app. Pet profiles, health records, weight entries, expenses, reminders and the photos you attach stay on your device.

2. Local storage and iCloud

By default, everything is stored locally. If you enable iCloud, your data syncs between your own devices through Apple's iCloud / CloudKit. That sync happens inside your own Apple account; the developer has no access to it and operates no server.

3. Device permissions

Pettrove may request the following permissions, only when you use the matching feature:

  • Camera / Photo Library — attaching a photo to a pet or record
  • Notifications — care and vaccination reminders

Reminders are generated by iOS local notifications; their content never passes through a server. Photos you import are stored locally or in your own iCloud.

4. Purchases handled by Apple

Pettrove Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited pets and export. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal. Purchases, restorations and refunds are processed by Apple; we never see your payment information.

5. App Store ratings and reviews

Pettrove may occasionally invite you to rate the app using Apple's built-in review prompt — for example after you log a weight entry. That prompt is displayed and handled entirely by Apple: we never learn whether you chose to rate the app, and we never receive your rating or the text of any review you write. Apple limits how often such prompts may appear, and we additionally limit them to at most once per app version.

The Settings screen also contains a link that opens the App Store page where you can write a review. That page belongs to and is operated by Apple; anything you submit there is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not this one.

If you contact us by email (for feedback or support), we receive only what you choose to write.

6. Third-party services

Pettrove does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, AI or tracking service, and uses no server of its own.

7. Children's privacy

Pettrove is not targeted at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Data security

All local data is protected by the standard iOS sandbox and indirectly by your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. You can clear all local data at any time by deleting the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the policy changes, with a new effective date. Continued use of the app implies acceptance of the updated terms.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email luanlzsn@gmail.com or contact the developer via the App Store listing.
